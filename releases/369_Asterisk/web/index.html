CONTROLS

  1. Install Asterisk 1.0 firmware. Power the Workshop Computer normally and connect its front USB-C port with a data cable.
  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge. Press Connect and allow MIDI and SysEx access.
  3. Set an internal tempo, mute voices with M, or isolate one or more voices with S. Mute wins over Solo.
  4. Click a voice to select it. Drag its name or symbol onto another voice to exchange their sounds. Their rhythms stay in place.
  5. Press the center disc for a new scene. Manual tempo, Delay Amount, Mute and Solo stay as set. Auto Tempo restores each scene’s generated tempo.

Delay Amount sets the overall echo volume: 0% silences the delay return, and 100% uses the generated level. It does not change the feedback or delay time.

External CV or USB MIDI clock takes priority over the tempo field. Existing echo tails can linger after muting. Closing this page leaves the current controls on the hardware; power cycling clears them.

Preview is a silent interface demo. Filter, Decay and Heat are controlled on the hardware.

Connection and audio diagnostics

Connect hardware to read its counters.

If audio underruns increase while playing, note the scene and operation and report them with your listening test.
