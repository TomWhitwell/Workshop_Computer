8mu buttons A-D, acted on when you let go. Short press: the page (press again for its next page: A, B and C have two, D three). Long press (half a second): the bank of steps the faders edit, A = 1-8, B = 9-16, C = 17-24, D = 25-32; that fader's LED flashes three times. Hold a button and move a fader (a good way, about a sixth of its travel): the last step, button = bank, fader = step within it (hold D + fader 8 = 32 steps, hold A + fader 4 = 4 steps). Here: click a row or use the bank buttons; set the length with − and +.

Shapes

Click a shape to give it to the selected step (orange outline), or to every selected step: as its shape 2 while the Shape 2 page is showing, otherwise as its shape 1. The selected step's shape 1 has a solid outline and its shape 2 a dashed one. Click a step on the timeline, or a fader, to select it.

Presets

    Presets live in this browser. Loading one sends it to the card if one is connected. The card itself keeps nothing over power-off.

    How it connects

    The card picks its USB mode at power-up. 8mu plugged into the card: the card reads it directly and this page isn't used. Computer plugged into the card: the card appears as a MIDI device called CVSeq, and an 8mu plugged into this computer is passed on by this page. After plugging a computer in, power-cycle the module.
    Help
    • Use Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser. Safari and iOS browsers can't do WebMIDI with SysEx.
    • Press Connect card & 8mu and allow MIDI with SysEx when the browser asks.
    • 8mu buttons A-D choose the page; pressing one again moves on to its next page (D has three: Level, Quant, Chance), then back to its first. The tabs here do the same. Faders pick up: one does nothing until it reaches the stored value (the dashed line shows where the 8mu's fader is while it waits).
    • On-screen faders: drag, or focus one and use the arrow keys. Double-click resets one.
    • Direction: hold the module's switch down and turn Main to choose one of the eight directions (tap without turning to step to the next), or use the buttons here.
    • The module's knobs: switch up = Main scale, X depth, Y offset; middle = Main rate, X smoothing, Y morph offset.
    • Tilting an 8mu forwards and back adds to every step's morph, as it would on the card. The first 15 degrees or so either side of flat are ignored, so holding it doesn't nudge the morph.
    • Without a card the page still edits, previews and keeps presets; the playhead is then simulated at one step a second, full depth, no offset.
