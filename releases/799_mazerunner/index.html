Maze Runner - Live Visualizer

Connects over USB-MIDI SysEx to a Workshop Computer running the Maze Runner card. Chrome or Edge required (Web MIDI API) - Safari and Firefox don't support it. Plug the card in over USB-C, then Connect below. Dual-path mode: Path A drives CV/Pulse Out 1, Path B drives CV/Pulse Out 2 - both solve the same maze independently and can diverge once braid % > 0. Generator, solvers, maze size and braid are "pending" until the card's switch is tapped - the yellow field shows what a tap would change to. Everything else updates live as you turn the card's own knobs.
