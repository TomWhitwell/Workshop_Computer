Wave bank

Click a wave to give it to the selected step (orange). Click a step on the timeline, or a fader, to select it. Steps can also sit between two waves, which morphs them.

Presets

    Presets live in this browser. Loading one sends it to the card if one is connected. The card itself keeps nothing over power-off.

    How it connects

    The card picks its USB mode at power-up. 8mu plugged into the card: the card reads it directly and this page isn't used. Computer plugged into the card: the card appears as a MIDI device called Wave Sequencer, and an 8mu plugged into this computer is passed on by this page. After plugging a computer in, power-cycle the module.
    Help
    • Use Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser. Safari and iOS browsers can't do WebMIDI with SysEx.
    • Press Connect card & 8mu and allow MIDI with SysEx when the browser asks.
    • 8mu buttons A-D (or the tabs) choose the page. Faders pick up: one does nothing until it reaches the stored value (the dashed line shows where the 8mu's fader is while it waits).
    • On-screen faders: drag, or focus one and use the arrow keys. Double-click resets one.
    • Direction: tap the module’s switch down to step forward, ping-pong, random, or use the buttons here.
    • The module’s X and Y knobs: switch up = speed and crossfade, middle = FM and wave scan amounts.
    • Tilting an 8mu plugged into this computer scans the waves and detunes Audio Out 2, as it would on the card.
    • Without a card the page still edits, previews and keeps presets; the playhead is then simulated at 1x speed.
