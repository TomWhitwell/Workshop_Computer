### 5. Resonant Filter

State-variable resonant filter (Lowpass to Highpass) with wavefolder drive fuzz.

#### Controls
- **Main (Cutoff)**: Cutoff frequency (Lowpass CCW to Highpass CW).
- **X (Resonance)**: Filter Q factor. Self-oscillates past 75%.
- **Y (Fuzz)**: Wavefolder drive fuzz level and saturation depth.
