### 4. Glitcher

Granular stutter looper playback, loop segment sizing, playback speed, and pitch sequence mutation.

#### Controls
- **Main (Glitch Mix)**: Wet/dry stutter looper blend.
- **X (Loop Size)**: Loop segment size (16 clock subdivisions when Clock Sync is patched).
- **Y (Speed & Pitch)**: Playback behavior and pitch mutation. Sweeps from simple repeat stutter (CCW), random reverse (Center), pitch shifting up/down with variable slice lengths (CW), to full buffer freeze (CW limit).
