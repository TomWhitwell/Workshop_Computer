### 2. Loss Engine

Morphs continuously between analog tape saturation, bitcrush fuzz, sample-rate decimation, tape wow/flutter, network dropouts, and CD sputter.

#### Controls
- **Main (Loss Mix)**: Wet/dry signal degradation and drive blend.
- **X (Crush)**: Morphs continuously across Tape Drive, MP3 Ringing, Bitcrush Fuzz, and Decimation.
- **Y (Jitter)**: Sweeps across Tape Wow & Flutter, Bad Connection dropouts, and CD Sputter skipping.
