### 6. Reverb Engine

Dual spring and hall reverb engine with plate damping, shimmer pitch-shifting, and circuit-bent jitter.

#### Controls
- **Main (Reverb Mix)**: Reverb wet/dry blend.
- **X (Reverb Size)**: Blends continuously from Spring Reverb (CCW) to Ambient Hall Reverb (CW).
- **Y (Damping)**: Damping, lo-fi degradation (>60%), shimmer pitch-shifting (>73%), and circuit jitter (>82%).
