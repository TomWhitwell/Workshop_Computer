### Freeze Mode (Switch UP)

Locks audio recording memory into RAM and enters live granular playhead scrubbing.

#### Controls
- **Main (Scrub Mode)**: Auto Sine LFO (<10%), Manual Scrub (10% to 90%), or Random Skip (>90%).
- **X (Slice Size)**: Granular slice length from micro-grains to full buffer loops.
- **Y (Pitch Shift)**: Continuous grain pitch shift with magnetic detents (-1st Octave, -5th, Unison, +5th, +1st Octave).
