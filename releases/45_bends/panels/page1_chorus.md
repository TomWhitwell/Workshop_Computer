### 1. Chorus / Tape Loss

Stereo BBD chorus, tape drift, BBD XOR data bus corruption, and Karplus-Strong string synthesis.

#### Controls
- **Main (Chorus Mix)**: Wet/dry chorus blend.
- **X (LFO Speed)**: Pitch modulation speed for tape drift, wow, and flutter.
- **Y (Depth / Feedback)**: Modulation depth (0% to 50%), resonant feedback (50% to 68%), Karplus-Strong string mode (68% to 96%), or bitwise BBD XOR distortion (96% to 100%).
