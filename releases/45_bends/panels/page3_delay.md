### 3. Multi-Tap Delay

Stereo multi-tap delay with 16 rhythmic clock subdivisions and feedback XOR overdrive.

#### Controls
- **Main (Delay Mix)**: Wet/dry delay blend.
- **X (Delay Time)**: Delay time. Selects 16 rhythmic subdivisions (1/16th note to 4 bars) when Clock Sync is patched. At longer delay times around 1 second, delay clock reduces quality for lo-fi character.
- **Y (Feedback)**: Repeat feedback level. Overdrives into self-oscillating digital XOR glitches past 70%.
