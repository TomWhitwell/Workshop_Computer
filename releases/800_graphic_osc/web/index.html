Connecting, and what to expect
  • Use a USB-C data cable (charge-only cables do not work), and close any other app that might be using the card or the 8mu (a serial monitor, another MIDI editor, another browser tab).
  • Use Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser. Safari and iOS browsers do not support WebMIDI with SysEx.
  • Press Connect card & 8mu. The browser asks for permission to use MIDI devices with SysEx: choose Allow. The card appears as Graphic Osc; an 8mu as 8mu.
  • Three ways to use it. (1) 8mu straight into the module: no computer needed, and this page is not used. (2) 8mu into this computer, this computer into the module: the 8mu drives this page and the page drives the card. (3) Only this computer: click and drag the faders.
  • The card decides once, at power-up, whether its USB port is a host (8mu) or a device (computer). After plugging the computer in, power-cycle the module.
  • The card forgets its settings when it is switched off. Presets live in this browser only; after a power-up, press Send to card or load a preset.
  • The preview is a re-implementation of the card's renderer. It shows the wave before symmetry, which the card applies while playing, and before band-limiting for pitch.
  • Faders: click and drag, or focus one and use the arrow keys (Shift = bigger steps). Double-click resets a fader.
