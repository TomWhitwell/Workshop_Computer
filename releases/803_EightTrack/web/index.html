Card presets

Eight presets kept in the card's own flash, which the card loads with the 8mu alone (hold A and move a fader), and saves (hold D and move a fader). At power-up the card loads the last one saved or loaded. Save puts the settings here into the slot.

Browser presets

    Presets in this browser, as many as you like. Click a name to load it (and send it to the card). → slot copies one into a card preset without loading it.

    Inputs & outputs

    Inputs
    Audio In 1, 2Left and right in
    CV In 1Added to X: +5 V adds ×1 to every head's volume and feedback
    CV In 2Added to Y: +5 V adds ×1 to every head's pan and width
    Pulse In 1Clock, a pulse a beat. A jack plugged in syncs the heads: to the clock from its second pulse, until then to the last beat it had (120 bpm at first)
    Pulse In 2Freeze, while high
    Outputs
    Audio Out 1, 2Left and right out, dry and wet as the Mix fader sets
    CV Out 1The tape's wow and flutter, ±5 V at full depth
    CV Out 2Envelope of the delayed signal, 0 to 5 V
    Pulse Out 1A trigger every beat (synced), or every free-time length (a fader at the top) when free
    Pulse Out 2High while frozen
    Panel
    MainFree: time multiplier, ×¼ to ×4, ×1 at the centre. Synced: ratio, /4 /3 /2 ×⅔ ×1 ×1½ ×2 ×3 ×4
    XVolume and feedback multiplier, ×0 to ×2, ×1 at the centre
    YPan and width multiplier, ×0 (mono, centred) to ×2
    Switch upFreeze
    Switch downTap tempo (momentary). Hold 1.5 s to go back to free time

    8mu

    The 8mu can plug into the module (no computer needed) or into this computer, which passes it on to the card. Its eight faders are the eight heads; its buttons are acted on when you let go.

    Short pressThe page. Press the same button again for its second page:
    A Time
    B Volume
    C Pan → Width
    D Feedback → FX
    Long press D
    (½ s)    		Freeze on or off
    Hold A + move
    a fader    		Load card preset 1-8 (the fader's number). The fader's LED flashes three times
    Hold D + move
    a fader    		Save the settings as card preset 1-8. The fader's LED blinks fast
    FadersPick up: after a page change a fader does nothing until it reaches (or passes) the stored value. While one is waiting, a dashed line on its on-screen fader shows where it is
    TiltForward and back bends every head's time up to an octave, past a dead zone of about 15°, like a hand on the reel

    How it connects

    The card picks its USB mode at power-up. 8mu plugged into the card: the card reads it directly and this page isn't used. Computer plugged into the card: the card appears as a MIDI device called EightTrack, and an 8mu plugged into this computer is passed on by this page. After plugging a computer in, power-cycle the module.
    Help
    • Use Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser. Safari and iOS browsers can't do WebMIDI with SysEx.
    • Press Connect card & 8mu and allow MIDI with SysEx when the browser asks.
    • On-screen faders: drag, or focus one and use the arrow keys. Double-click resets one to its default.
    • Ping-pong: give two heads a width of 0 (so each takes the mono sum), pan one hard left and one hard right, and give them feedback.
    • Freeze loops the tape at the length of the longest head that's playing; the heads keep moving over it, so changing times while frozen slides around the loop.
    • Without a card the page still edits and keeps presets; the tape picture then shows free time at ×1.
