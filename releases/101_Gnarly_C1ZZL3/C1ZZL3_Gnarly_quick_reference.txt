C1ZZL3 Gnarly Quick Reference
=============================

Full user guide: README.md
Standard firmware: uf2/C1ZZL3_GNARLY_V11.uf2
8mu v2 firmware: uf2/Gnarly_C1ZZL3_8mu.uf2
Firmware checksums: uf2/SHA256SUMS.txt
Flash one firmware image at a time.

Panel controls (both firmware choices)
--------------------------------------
Switch middle: Main = shared pitch; X = oscillator 1 PD; Y = oscillator 1 recipe.
Switch up: Main = oscillator 2 interval (-24 to +24 semitones around unison);
           X = oscillator 2 PD; Y = oscillator 2 recipe.
Switch down: Main = recipe bank; X = ring modulation; Y = noise/grit.
Hold down from middle for about eight seconds to save the startup settings.

Audio/CV In 1: shared 1V/oct pitch modulation.
CV In 1: phase-distortion modulation for both oscillators.
CV In 2: recipe-slot/wave modulation for both oscillators.
Pulse In 2: envelope trigger; held gates loop supported envelopes, gate-off releases.
Audio Out 1/2: oscillator 1/2. Pulse In 1 is unused; CV and pulse outputs stay low.

Recipe banks
------------
1: Simple wave families, with morphing between waves.
2: Warm compound pairings.
3: Bright resonant/windowed pairings.
4: CZ-style pairings.
Compound blend weights vary: first-heavy, equal, or second-heavy.
See README.md for the complete recipe table.

LEDs
----
LEDs are numbered left-to-right, top-to-bottom: 1 2 / 3 4 / 5 6.
On the down page, LEDs 1/2 encode recipe bank: off/off, on/off, off/on, on/on.
On oscillator pages, LEDs 1/2 show the active oscillator's PD and recipe position.
LED 3: oscillator 2 interval distance from unison; LEDs 4/5: ring/noise amount.
Standard firmware LED 6: middle = half brightness, up = off, down = full.
8mu firmware LED 6: levels = steady on; times = blinking twice per second.
Startup selection and save confirmation temporarily take priority.

8mu controller banks
--------------------
Use the 8mu firmware and import the eight numbered JSON files from 8mu_banks/.
Choose the matching destination bank and click Update controller after each import.
Sliders use MIDI CCs; buttons use Keyboard mode, channel 1, velocity 127.
Bank 1: performance controls; button notes 124-127 are ignored.
Banks 2-7: Amp1, Amp2, PD1, PD2, Pitch1, Pitch2; sliders 1-8 edit stages 1-8.
Bank 8: slider 1 selects the envelope/custom slot; sliders 2-7 set
        Amp1/PD1/Pitch1/Pitch2/PD2/Amp2 depth; slider 8 sets master depth.
In banks 2-8: A = note 120, level mode; B = note 121, time mode;
             C = note 122, save all six lanes and current performance settings;
             D = note 123, revert all six lanes to their saved values.
Button releases do not change the selected mode. These notes do not play pitches.
Stage editing and save/revert require a saved custom slot; factory slots are read-only.
Depth controls are temporary and are not stored by C. Gestures are disabled.

MIDI performance controls (both firmware choices)
------------------------------------------------
CC1 / CC27: oscillator 1 PD
CC20: oscillator 1 recipe; CC21: oscillator 2 recipe
CC22: ring; CC23: recipe bank; CC24: oscillator 2 interval
CC25: oscillator 2 PD; CC26: noise/grit
8mu firmware also accepts CC120-123 as button actions for other controllers.

Web MIDI
--------

https://computer.musicthing.co.uk/programs/101-gnarly-c1zzl3/web/index.html

https://computer.musicthing.co.uk/programs/101-gnarly-c1zzl3/web/import/index.html
