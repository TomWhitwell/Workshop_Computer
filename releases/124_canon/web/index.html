The Rule of the Card

  • Z up — play
  • Z middle — stop
  • Hold Z down + turn Main (when stopped) — choose preset 1–12; let go to keep it
  • LEDs when stopped: the active preset. One LED lit = presets 1–6; all lit but one = presets 7–12.
  • X and Y do nothing.

Internal tempo
