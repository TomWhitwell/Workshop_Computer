WS-DOOM

Doom as a program card for the Music Thing Modular Workshop System Computer. The whole game runs on the module's RP2040 — no screen, no PC. You play it from the panel and listen your way through: the stereo mix pans enemies left and right, and the music itself swells toward the nearest one, a beacon you can hunt by ear.

Knobs steer and pick weapons, the Z switch fires (up = auto-aim), CV outs carry health and ammo, pulse outs trigger on shots and damage, and the LEDs are an enemy radar. Patch audio in 1 to replace the soundtrack with your own; audio in 2 gates open on every shot.

This page is optional — connect the module over USB-C and press Connect to stream the actual game video onto the CRT. The full panel mapping is in the sidebar.
