Gnarly C1ZZL3 8mu Version Quick Reference
==========================================

Release: 109 / Gnarly C1ZZL3 8mu v1
UF2: uf2/Gnarly_C1ZZL3_8mu.uf2

Important identity
------------------
- Gnarly is the no-Turing C1ZZL3 version.
- Core remains the simpler production C1ZZL3 card 84.
- Rad can be added to card 84 later as the advanced-with-Turing version.

Switch middle: oscillator 1
---------------------------
Main: shared pitch
X: oscillator 1 phase distortion
Y: oscillator 1 recipe slot
Audio/CV In 1: 1V/oct pitch
CV In 1: adds phase distortion
CV In 2: adds recipe-slot/wave modulation
Pulse In 2: trigger envelopes; held gate loops/sustains; gate-off releases

Switch up: oscillator 2
-----------------------
Main: oscillator 2 interval/spread, centred at unison
X: oscillator 2 phase distortion
Y: oscillator 2 recipe slot

Switch down hold: performance and bank
--------------------------------------
Main: recipe bank
X: ring modulation
Y: noise/grit
Hold switch down from middle to save current performance settings.

Recipe banks
------------
Bank 1: simple single-wave families
Bank 2: warm compound pairings
Bank 3: bright resonant/windowed pairings
Bank 4: odd/import-faithful CZ-style pairings

Recipe slot examples
--------------------
Bank 1: Saw, Square, Narrow pulse, Double sine, Saw pulse, Resonant saw, Resonant triangle, Resonant trapezoid
Bank 2: Double sine + Saw, Double sine + Square, Saw + Double sine, Square + Double sine, Narrow pulse + Double sine, Saw pulse + Double sine, Resonant triangle + Double sine, Resonant trapezoid + Double sine
Bank 3: Resonant saw + Saw, Resonant triangle + Square, Resonant trapezoid + Narrow pulse, Resonant saw + Saw pulse, Resonant triangle + Saw pulse, Resonant trapezoid + Saw pulse, Resonant saw + Narrow pulse, Resonant trapezoid + Square
Bank 4: Saw pulse + Double sine, Resonant triangle + Double sine, Saw pulse + Narrow pulse, Resonant trapezoid + Double sine, Resonant saw + Saw pulse, Narrow pulse + Resonant trapezoid, Square + Resonant saw, Resonant triangle + Narrow pulse

Recipes are weighted blends, not exact 50/50 mixes.

Bank LEDs on switch-down page
-----------------------------
Bank 1: LEDs 1 and 2 off
Bank 2: LED 1 on
Bank 3: LED 2 on
Bank 4: LEDs 1 and 2 on

MIDI CC
-------
CC1: oscillator 1 phase distortion, mod-wheel friendly
CC20: oscillator 1 recipe slot
CC21: oscillator 2 recipe slot
CC22: ring modulation
CC23: recipe bank
CC24: oscillator 2 interval/spread
CC25: oscillator 2 phase distortion
CC26: noise/grit
CC27: oscillator 1 phase distortion for eight-knob controllers

Music Thing 8mu factory faders
------------------------------
Fader 1 / CC34: Amp1 envelope depth
Fader 2 / CC35: PD1 envelope depth
Fader 3 / CC36: Pitch1 envelope depth
Fader 4 / CC37: Pitch2 envelope depth
Fader 5 / CC38: PD2 envelope depth
Fader 6 / CC39: Amp2 envelope depth
Fader 7 / CC40: select Off, factory envelope, or saved sound-preset slot
Fader 8 / CC41: master depth for all envelope lanes

Full depth reproduces the Web MIDI envelope. Zero removes Amp/PD modulation or
centres pitch. Fader moves are live and do not rewrite saved envelopes.
