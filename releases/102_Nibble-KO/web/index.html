Inputs

CV in 1 — Four Voltages output 1 — the instrument itself. Calibrated on an alt-boot and saved to flash.

CV in 2 — takes over the Main knob while patched, so whatever Main does in the current mode is under voltage. Overrides rather than offsets — a cable wins.

Pulse in 1 — external clock, one edge per beat

Pulse in 2 — while HIGH, a random effect is applied. The gate's width is how long it lasts; it sits below your hands and below anything recorded.

Audio in 1 — depth of that random effect

Audio in 2 — chaos — how busy and how fine the glitch outputs are. Unpatched sits at about 5%.

Outputs

Audio out 1 & 2 — the drum bus, mono, both jacks identical

CV out 1 — glitch gates, sparse and weighted toward the downbeat — usable as an accent track on its own

CV out 2 — glitch gates, dense and syncopated. Patch it into Pulse in 2 and the card glitches itself.

Pulse out 1 — gate, one pulse per hit

Pulse out 2 — click, one pulse per beat

Knobs

Main / X / Y — DJ filter (or FX depth) / tempo 40–240bpm / kit character

NIBBLE-KO Setup

Connecting

  • On the card: hold the switch Down and press B + D together
  • All four pads glow slowly — the card is now on USB and silent
  • Click Connect USB and allow MIDI access when the browser asks

Assigning sounds

  • Every gesture can play any sound: its own synth voice, a built-in sample, or one of yours
  • Changes are heard immediately — no reboot, so you can audition while you pick
  • Click Save Kit to Card to keep them. That writes flash, which restarts the card — press switch+B+D and Connect again to carry on
  • One sample can be played by several voices at once, and costs the space of one

The sample library

  • On the Samples tab, click Add Samples… and pick one or more WAVs — any rate or channel count
  • They are converted to 8-bit mono 48kHz, trimmed, loudness-matched and dithered, exactly as tools/importwav.py does for the built-in ones
  • Click Upload Staged. The card goes silent, writes flash, and restarts
  • Uploads append, so adding one sample leaves the rest alone. Deleting frees the slot but not the bytes — only Erase All reclaims space

Getting back to playing

  • An upload reboots the card by itself
  • Otherwise just power-cycle it — the saved calibration survives, so it comes straight back up playable

Patching the card

  • CV In 2 takes over the Main knob while patched — the DJ filter in Drums and Mute, the held effect's depth in FX. A cable wins, the same way an external clock overrides the tempo knob
  • Pulse In 2, while high, applies a random effect for exactly as long as the gate lasts — a new effect is rolled each time it goes high. Audio In 1 sets that effect's depth
  • Audio In 2 is chaos: how often and how finely CV Out 1 (sparse, beat-anchored) and CV Out 2 (dense, syncopated) glitch. Both follow whatever quantise grid is live on the card
  • Patch CV Out 2 into Pulse In 2 and the card fires random effects at itself — the payoff patch, and the best single test that this is all working
  • None of this is configured here; it is fixed in firmware and playable the moment you patch a cable. See the project README for the fuller explanation
