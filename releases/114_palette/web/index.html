Bits move left to right on each step. The bit leaving a loop's right end comes back at its left end, flipped at random by Main and the loop's chaos.
Drag a chip from the tray onto the grid to assign it. Drag a bar to move it, its ends to resize it, or back to the tray to unassign it. Drag on an empty lane to draw a range. Click a state bit to flip it.

Inputs & outputs

Inputs
CV In 1Pitch, 1 V/oct, quantised to semitones
CV In 2Added to Main (randomness)
Pulse In 1Clock; Y sets an internal clock when unplugged
Pulse In 2Fires both envelopes
Audio In 1, 2Not used
Outputs
Audio Out 1The voice
Audio Out 2Filter envelope, 0 to about +6 V
CV Out 1Osc 1 pitch, calibrated 1 V/oct (MIDI note 60 = 0 V), with glide
CV Out 2Volume envelope, 0 to about +6 V
Pulse Out 1Trigger whenever the envelopes fire
Pulse Out 2Trigger on every clock step

Panel

MainRandomness: noon random, fully clockwise locked, fully anticlockwise locked and inverting (repeats at twice the loop length)
XTranspose, 0 to +24 semitones
YInternal clock, about 1 to 16 steps a second
Pick upAfter this page sets a knob's value, the knob does nothing until it reaches (or passes) that value, so nothing jumps. The dotted box and the orange line show a knob that is waiting, and where it is; LED 5 blinks fast on the module.
SwitchUp locks every loop; middle runs; down scrambles every bit inside a loop
LEDsLEDs 0-3 show register bits 0-3, LED 4 the clock, LED 5 the volume envelope (blinking fast while a knob is waiting)
