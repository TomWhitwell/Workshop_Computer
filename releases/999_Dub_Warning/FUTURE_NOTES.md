# Dub Warning Future Notes

## Current direction

The shippable version is now a single PT2399-style dub siren. Separate reverb
experiments were tested and removed because the PT2399 repeats already smear
into a useful reverb-like wash at high settings.

## Possible future changes

- Tune the Wheel Up one-shot phrase after more listening on a sound system.
- Add a simple pre-delay high-pass or tone-button color before the delay.
- Consider using Audio In 2 as an external delay feedback/send input.
- Consider a smoother sine-like oscillator color at the low end of CV In 2.
