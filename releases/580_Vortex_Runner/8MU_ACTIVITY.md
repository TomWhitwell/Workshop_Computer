Use the **8mu edition**, `uf2/Vortex_Runner_8mu_0.1.2.uf2`. The standard
edition does not provide these controller layers. A–D select layers and do not
save or revert presets. Both editions share source and build instructions at
[Building Both Editions](README.md#building-both-editions).
